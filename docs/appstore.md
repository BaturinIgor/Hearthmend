## Описание (en)
Bring a forgotten village back to life, one merge at a time.

Merge seeds into pumpkins, twigs into benches and pebbles into golden lanterns. Fill the villagers' orders, earn stars and rebuild the square, the farmstead and the riverside, building by building, in charming pixel art.

• Relaxing merge-2 puzzle with no timers that punish you
• Rebuild a whole village: well, inn, market, windmill, smithy and more
• New neighbours move in and bring new merge chains
• Firefly jar: energy never goes to waste while you're away
• Weekend festivals with a story choice: whose wish will you grant?
• Decorate the village your way: place event decorations anywhere
• Daily gifts, levels and cozy music
