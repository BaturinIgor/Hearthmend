Hearthmend Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Hearthmend does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

Game progress

Your progress is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the progress.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, to give you the item.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Политика конфиденциальности Hearthmend

Hearthmend не собирает, не хранит и не передает персональные данные. В приложении нет аккаунтов, аналитики и рекламы.

Прогресс игры хранится только на вашем устройстве. Покупки обрабатывает Apple через App Store, данные оплаты нам недоступны.
