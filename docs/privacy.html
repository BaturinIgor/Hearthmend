Hearthmend Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary

Hearthmend does not collect your name, email, contacts or any other personal data. There are no accounts and no advertising. Starting with version 1.2 the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game; you can turn them off in Settings.

Anonymous statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played: which tutorial steps are finished, levels and buildings reached, which screens are opened, when energy or gems run out, and purchases (product and price, not payment details). Firebase also receives a random app-instance identifier, the device model, the iOS version, the app version and the approximate country derived from the network.

The statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. The advertising identifier (IDFA) and the vendor identifier (IDFV) are not collected. Data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

To stop sending statistics, open Settings in the game and turn off “Send anonymous stats”. Firebase data stored on the device is then deleted.

Messages to the developer

If you write to us from Settings, your message is delivered together with the game version, iOS version, device model and your game level, so we can help. Messages are used only to answer you and improve the game. If you write by email, we see your email address.

Game progress

Your progress is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the progress.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, to give you the item.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous statistics described above contain no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Политика конфиденциальности Hearthmend

Hearthmend не собирает имя, почту, контакты и другие персональные данные. Аккаунтов и рекламы нет. С версии 1.2 приложение отправляет анонимную статистику игры в Google Analytics for Firebase: шаги обучения, уровни, постройки, открытые экраны, нехватку энергии и покупки (товар и цена). Вместе с ней уходят случайный идентификатор установки, модель устройства, версия iOS и примерная страна. IDFA и IDFV не собираются, статистика не связана с вашей личностью и не используется для рекламы. Выключить: Настройки в игре → «Анонимная статистика».

Сообщения разработчику из настроек приходят вместе с версией игры и iOS, моделью устройства и уровнем.

Прогресс игры хранится только на вашем устройстве. Покупки обрабатывает Apple через App Store, данные оплаты нам недоступны.
